I am a third-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at [IMT Atlantique](https://www.imt-atlantique.fr/en) within the [BRoader Artificial Intelligence (BRAIN)](http://recherche.imt-atlantique.fr/brain/) team.

My research focuses on **Masked Models** including:
- **Diffusion-based Generative Models**: Discrete diffusion decoding, parallel sampling, and diversity mechanisms.
- **EEG Foundation Models**: Large-scale pretraining and spatial-temporal representations (e.g., [REVE](https://brain-bzh.github.io/reve/) at NeurIPS 2025).
- Other topics covering **parameter-efficient fine-tuning**, **computer vision** and **few-shot learning**.

I was previously a Visiting Scientist at **Sony Europe** (Stuttgart, Germany) and a Research Intern at **Criteo AI Lab** (Paris, France).
